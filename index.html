About

I’m a technology founder and consultant. My work spans AI/ML, platform engineering, product strategy, architecture, and go-to-market. I enjoy the whole process: understanding a problem, building the software, and figuring out how to make it useful to people.

I’ve worked at IBM, JPMorgan Chase, and Cerence, which was spun off from Nuance Communications.

Today, I’m building Synergius AI and Synergius Flow. The question behind both is simple: how can AI help get enterprise work done, with people still able to review decisions and control what happens next?

What I’m building

Synergius AI

Small models trained for specific business domains, backed by relevant enterprise knowledge. The focus is practical: classifying requests, extracting information, routing work, and recommending next steps. On-premise and air-gapped deployment options help keep sensitive data within the organisation.

Synergius Flow

The orchestration layer that connects AI with the work around it: systems, tasks, dependencies, schedules, and people. Workflows can pause for approval, recover from failures, and keep a record of each step.

  • Connect existing systems. Bring AI into enterprise tools, databases, and APIs.
  • Keep people involved. Put review and approval where the decision warrants it.
  • Make execution traceable. Record inputs, actions, outcomes, and failures.
